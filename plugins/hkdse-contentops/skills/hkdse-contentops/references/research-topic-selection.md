## Research scope

Use this workflow for today's content, current news, the latest policy, hot topics, current requirements, evidence-led calendars, or recent parent/student concerns.

Use the user's local date and timezone. Search official updates from the last 30 days, expand to 90 days in slow periods, prioritize local context and platform demand from the last 14–30 days, and map the current DSE seasonal stage. Do not force every layer into one post.

## Source hierarchy

Use the source that owns the fact:

1. HKEAA for HKDSE examination arrangements, statistics, and official exam information.
2. JUPAS for JUPAS procedures, dates, and system rules.
3. Hong Kong Education Bureau for education policy.
4. The relevant university or institution for programme requirements and admissions information.
5. The relevant school, examination body, or programme owner for its own rules.
6. Reputable Hong Kong reporting for context and public discussion, not authority over the owner.
7. Instagram, Xiaohongshu, Threads, forums, search suggestions, comments, and other social sources for audience language and demand signals only.

Cross-check a material claim with a second primary source when it spans two owners. Never let a viral creator, tutoring account, news headline, or comment override primary information.

## Platform routing

### Instagram

Research local Hong Kong demand in Traditional Chinese, written Cantonese, and common English subject terms. When accessible, inspect recent DSE-focused Instagram profiles, Reels, carousels, captions, public comments, repeated cover structures, student situations, and share/save-oriented formats. Supplement sparse comments with related public Threads, local forum, video, or search discussion.

Instagram user search is not post-keyword search. Do not claim a user-search result proves a topic is trending. If direct post discovery is limited, use focused web search, known relevant profiles, and public-page inspection; label the limitation.

### Xiaohongshu

Search at least two intent families when tools permit:

- broad: `香港DSE`, `DSE备考`, `DSE家长`;
- stage/topic: `DSE选科`, `JUPAS`, `中五升中六`, `DSE放榜`, or the current seasonal issue.

Read a small representative set of relevant notes and comments. Keep the Simplified-Mandarin audience lens.

### No platform specified

Research official and Hong Kong context once. Sample both platform environments when tools permit. Select one shared audience problem, then adapt natively:

- Instagram: local students first, Traditional Cantonese, fast recognition, save/share, and a Reel/carousel/Story loop;
- Xiaohongshu: Mandarin parents and students, searchable explanation, decision support, and a note/video/live loop.

Do not claim the same demand signal exists equally on both platforms without evidence.

## Daily workflow

1. Identify the platform route, target stage, date, and seasonal DSE moment.
2. Search primary official sources for material updates.
3. Read relevant Hong Kong reporting only for context, confusion, and reaction.
4. Sample the chosen platform's recent content and search language; record dates and URLs.
5. Inspect comments on one to three useful items when available.
6. Separate parent concerns from student concerns.
7. Generate three to five original topic candidates.
8. Score candidates and choose one for qualified acquisition rather than empty reach.
9. Assign format roles: Reel for discovery, carousel/note for depth, Story/live for interaction or conversion.
10. Draft with current-claim labels and official sources where required.

Treat likes, comments, shares, and saves as directional. They are not directly comparable across accounts, formats, audience sizes, and publication dates.

Never claim `全网最火` or `家长最关心` from a small sample. Say `近期样本中反复出现`, `当前搜索样本显示`, or `抽查的公开评论中多次出现`.

## Parent/student concern mining

Classify concerns:

- eligibility: DSE eligibility or application route;
- decision: subject, programme, school, pathway, JUPAS, or post-results action;
- performance: weak subjects, practice, writing, timing, revision, or repeated errors;
- process: dates, documents, applications, results-day procedures, and official steps;
- emotion: anxiety, procrastination, parent-child conflict, comparison, and confidence;
- trust: whether a statistic, score line, viral claim, course, or promise is credible;
- conversion: what help the family wants before taking a next step.

Prefer questions answerable with a checklist, worked example, diagnostic framework, comparison, timeline, or next action. For student-led Instagram, favor daily situations and direct actions. For parent-led content, favor decisions, communication, verification, and process clarity.

## Topic scoring

Score candidates from 0–5 for timeliness, concern intensity, discovery fit, actionability, save/share potential, visual potential, acquisition fit, and evidence strength. Subtract 0–5 for uncertainty, sensationalism, privacy, mismatch, or compliance risk.

Use:

`total = timeliness + concern + discovery + actionability + save_share + visual + acquisition + evidence - risk`

Use the formula as a decision aid, not fake precision. Break ties with stronger primary evidence, a clearer audience, and a more natural platform format.

## Failure and fallback rules

- No official update: state `未发现需要今天紧急解读的重大官方更新`, then choose a seasonal evergreen topic.
- Instagram post discovery unavailable: use focused public-web inspection, relevant profiles, and local discussion; state the limitation.
- Xiaohongshu unavailable: use available web/social search and state that platform sampling was unavailable.
- Comments unavailable: infer questions only from search phrasing and public summaries; label them as hypotheses.
- Conflicting official information: do not draft a definitive claim; name the owner to recheck.
- Stale viral content: do not revive it as current without a present-day reason.
- Small sample: do not generalize to all students, parents, or the platform.
- Research tool failure: do not fabricate findings or pretend browsing succeeded.

## Research brief format

```markdown
## 今日研判

> 资料截至：YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM（时区）

- 平台与受众：...
- 官方动态：...
- 香港本地语境：...
- 平台需求信号：...
- 家长/学生高频问题：...
- 候选选题：A / B / C
- 今日主选题：...
- 选择理由：时效 + 实用价值 + 平台形式 + 获客承接
- 研究限制：无则写“无重大限制”
```

Keep the brief factual and concise. The publishable copy must not read like a research report.
