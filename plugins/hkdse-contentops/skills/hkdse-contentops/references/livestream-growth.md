## Rule verification

Before giving current platform-rule advice, verify the latest rules through Xiaohongshu's current rule center or platform rule accounts when accessible. Add a cutoff date. Separate verified hard rules from operating recommendations.

At minimum, protect against:

- absolute, authoritative, or guaranteed-result claims;
- private contact details or external commercial diversion in speech, visuals, profile, comments, or pinned content;
- fabricated scarcity, price, results, testimonials, or student cases;
- prerecorded or stolen content presented as live and delayed interaction;
- unauthorized music, videos, textbooks, papers, or other copyrighted material;
- improper dress, unsafe environment, personal-data exposure, or deceptive AI simulation.

When AI contributes materially, direct the operator to use the current platform disclosure controls.

## People-product-place

### People

- Host: credible DSE teacher or planner who can teach and diagnose without overclaiming.
- Moderator: classify comments, surface questions, protect privacy, operate only approved platform components, and log timing/data.
- Operator, when available: monitor audio, lighting, online trend, retention, and segment transitions.

For a solo stream, prepare a visible timing card and comment monitor. Do not let the host stare down or ignore comments for long periods.

### Product

Use a three-layer offer:

1. free knowledge: framework taught in the stream;
2. free public tool: checklist or self-diagnostic shown in-platform;
3. paid or consultative service: only an actual, approved platform listing with accurate scope and price.

Do not invent a course, consultation, price, discount, availability, or refund promise.

### Place

Use a clean teaching setup: eye-level half-body camera, clear microphone, soft front light, simple account/topic board, and a whiteboard or keyword cards. Remove phone numbers, QR codes, external-app handles, private student records, and unauthorized materials from the frame.

## Pre-live sequence

- T-7 to T-3: publish a search-focused post and collect one safe question such as grade plus subject.
- T-2 to T-1: publish a short video that exposes the problem and previews the framework.
- T-0: verify equipment, backdrop, approved components, facts, prices, moderator responses, and blocked privacy terms.

Use one stable weekly livestream slot only as a recommendation unless the user supplies a schedule.

## Run-of-show

For a 60–90 minute stream, include exact timing:

1. 0:00–0:30: audience, problem, and concrete takeaway.
2. 0:30–3:00: agenda and first easy comment prompt.
3. 3:00–10:00: common scenario and misconception.
4. 10:00–55:00: two to four teaching modules with examples and questions.
5. 55:00–65:00: clearly labelled hypothetical demonstration or consented real example.
6. 65:00–75:00: restrained offer or next in-platform step.
7. 75:00–85:00: prioritized Q&A.
8. 85:00–90:00: summary, next topic, and compliant CTA.

Add a 15–25 second re-entry line every 8–10 minutes for new viewers: identity, topic, value, and current comment question.

## Script requirements

Return:

- livestream title and cover copy;
- audience and one promised takeaway;
- people-product-place checklist;
- pre-live content sequence;
- timed run-of-show;
- verbatim opening, transitions, teaching script, re-entry lines, Q&A, offer, and close;
- at least three easy interaction questions;
- moderator cues and pinned-question guidance;
- prohibited-claim replacements;
- post-live actions and metrics.

Keep the host's language natural Mandarin. Do not make the host read compliance disclaimers repeatedly; state limitations where the relevant claim appears.

## Comment moderation

Classify comments:

- green: general subject, planning, or stage questions—answer or group by theme;
- yellow: individual score, admission, policy, or personal-case questions—request only non-identifying context and avoid definitive conclusions;
- red: private contact details, guarantees, personal attacks, private student data, or requests to bypass the platform—hide, redirect, or handle through current platform controls.

Safe responses:

- `一个分数不能替所有课程下结论，需要核对具体课程的最新官方要求。`
- `不能保证提分或录取，我们可以帮助识别问题并建立执行计划。`
- `为了保护信息和交易安全，只使用小红书平台内支持的合规方式。`

## Compliant conversion

Without an approved listing:

- ask users to save, follow, answer a safe question, or set the next livestream reminder.

With an actual approved listing:

- explain who it is for, what it includes, what it does not promise, and that scope/price follow the live platform page;
- invite users to review fit before deciding;
- never create pressure with fake countdowns or unverified limited availability.

## Post-live loop

Within 24 hours:

- record peak and segment-level audience changes, retention, meaningful comments, follows, approved-component clicks, and conversions when available;
- turn the top question into one graphic post and one 30–45 second clip;
- answer general comments; verify current individual claims before responding;
- schedule the next content based on repeated questions, not the loudest single comment.

Change one major variable per test: topic, cover, opening hook, teaching order, interaction question, or offer timing. Use the account's own baseline instead of universal performance guarantees.
