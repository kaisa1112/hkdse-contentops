## Audience and language

Write primarily for local Hong Kong F4–F6 students. Treat parents as a secondary audience unless the topic is explicitly parent-led.

Use Traditional Chinese and natural Hong Kong written Cantonese as a hard requirement:

- prefer `溫書、操卷、放榜、選科、補習、大學、校內試、失分位、追進度`;
- avoid Mainland-native terms such as `高考、刷題、院校、上岸` unless quoting or explaining them;
- use standard Traditional Chinese for official facts and Cantonese for hooks, examples, captions, and spoken scripts;
- retain familiar English terms such as Past Paper, Mock, SBA, Band A, Level, Topic Sentence, Physics, Chem, Bio, and Math when locally natural;
- never convert a Mandarin Xiaohongshu draft character-by-character and present it as Instagram copy.

Default to a useful, neutral DSE media voice when no institution is supplied.

## Daily output contract

Create one coherent daily campaign. Use the same audience problem across the carousel, Reel, and Stories, but give each format a different job:

- Reel: stop the scroll and create problem recognition;
- carousel: deliver the saveable framework, checklist, example, or decision tool;
- Stories: collect a low-friction response and route users to the related asset or approved path;
- caption: deepen the context, state limitations, and ask one clear next action.

Return the target stage, selected topic and evidence type, complete carousel, complete Reel, three to five Stories, posting order, one A/B variable, likely comments, and the next FAQ asset.

## Carousel contract

Default to a 4:5 canvas such as 1080×1350 px. Return:

1. audience and funnel purpose;
2. cover title in one to three short lines;
3. seven to nine complete pages;
4. copy and layout instructions for every page;
5. publish-ready Traditional-Cantonese caption;
6. one primary CTA;
7. five to eight relevant hashtags;
8. one A/B cover variable when requested.

Use this page sequence:

1. Cover: audience/situation + specific conflict or result.
2. Recognition: familiar wrong approach without shaming.
3. Diagnosis: why it fails or who is affected.
4–6. Framework: one concrete step per page with an example.
7. Action tool: checklist, table, timeline, error code, or decision path.
8. Summary: one sentence and one CTA.
9. Optional conversion page: only for a real supplied service or approved resource.

Keep each page to one conclusion and three to six short lines. Use explicit labels such as `錯誤一、第一步、例子、今晚可以做` and useful structures such as red/yellow/green sorting, K/C/T/Q error codes, before/after answers, seven-day calendars, and comparison tables.

### Reusable carousel patterns

- `中五升中六最易做錯嘅3件事`
- `每日溫3個鐘，點解成績冇郁？`
- `Past Paper做極都冇進步？你漏咗呢一步`
- `錯題簿唔係抄答案就得`
- `中文閱讀寫好多，點解都唔中Point？`
- `JUPAS Band A應該點樣排？`
- `得返7日，仲可以點救？`
- `唔好再只問小朋友今日溫咗幾耐`

Vary the structure and examples. Do not repeatedly publish numbered mistakes with different nouns.

## Visual design system

If no brand system is supplied, recommend electric blue for structure, warm yellow for one emphasis, red only for warnings, deep navy for text, off-white backgrounds, one Traditional-Chinese sans-serif family, and one clean Latin font for numbers or English terms.

Use a large cover title, consistent inner headings, phone-readable body text, generous margins, and limited emphasis. Use functional student visuals: answer sheets, timers, calendars, traffic-light labels, error tags, highlighters, sticky notes, subject tabs, JUPAS comparison cards, and progress bars.

Use diagrams only for processes, comparisons, sequences, or decisions. Avoid fake result slips, fabricated testimonials, generic campus stock, excessive teacher portraits, and irrelevant formulas.

Before handoff, check that the visual and caption discuss the same topic.

## Reels contract

Default to 9:16, such as 1080×1920 px, and 20–40 seconds. Return:

1. audience and funnel purpose;
2. cover title in one or two short lines;
3. exact 0–2 second hook;
4. 20–40 second verbatim local Cantonese script;
5. beat-by-beat timing, shot, gesture, prop, B-roll, and subtitle cues;
6. caption that adds context;
7. one primary CTA;
8. five to eight relevant hashtags;
9. one alternate hook when requested.

Use this rhythm:

- 0–2s: exact audience situation or conflict; no account introduction.
- 2–7s: why the familiar approach fails.
- 7–28s: two to four steps or one worked example.
- final 3–6s: summary and one low-friction action.

Change the visual beat every 1.5–3 seconds through camera angle, crop, prop, B-roll, example, or text emphasis. Burn Traditional-Chinese subtitles into the draft and keep them away from interface edges. Do not make the presenter read citations, hashtags, or headings aloud.

### Reusable Reel structures

- Stop doing this: replace one ineffective action.
- One-question diagnosis: route three answer types.
- Before/after answer: revise a weak answer line by line.
- Timer method: show a 20–40-minute sequence.
- Command-word breakdown: demonstrate `Explain/Compare/Suggest`.
- Decision warning: correct a JUPAS or subject-choice misconception.
- Exam-night checklist: protect stable performance.
- Comment reply: answer one non-identifying question.

## Hook and topic systems

Useful formulas:

- `如果你而家係中五升中六，先停一停。`
- `每日溫三個鐘都冇進步？問題可能唔係時間。`
- `Past Paper愈做愈低分，先睇你係邊一類錯。`
- `英文作文第一句仲寫Nowadays？`
- `明明識答，點解長題最後得一兩分？`
- `JUPAS排位之前，唔好只睇總分。`
- `得返七日，最唔應該做嘅係由第一課重新開始。`
- `家長每日問“溫咗幾耐”，未必問中真正問題。`

Do not use `一定、保證、必入、穩入、最易攞分、人人適用` without defensible qualification. Do not copy a viral hook word for word.

## Captions, hashtags, Stories, and CTAs

Structure captions as: deepen the hook, explain one reason or limitation, give one action for today, and end with one CTA. For current facts, add `資料截至：YYYY-MM-DD` and official sources.

Use five to eight hashtags mixing a broad niche (`#香港DSE`), stage, subject/topic, intent, and optional account-owned series tag. Do not stuff unrelated trend tags.

Create three to five Story frames: recognition poll/quiz, useful answer, feed handoff, optional safe question box, and optional approved conversion action. Safe questions include `你而家係F4、F5定F6？`, `你最多係知識、Careless定時間問題？`, and `下一條想睇中文、英文定Math？`.

Use one CTA per asset. Cold users save/share; engaged users answer one safe question or view a Highlight; high-intent users may DM a keyword or use an actual profile link only when supplied and approved. Never invent a guide, automated DM, WhatsApp number, booking link, course, price, or deadline.

## Quality checks

Verify local readable language, matching cover/pages/script/caption/CTA, concrete examples, save value, a strong first two seconds, dated official claims, one real privacy-safe CTA, and complete designer/presenter handoff.
