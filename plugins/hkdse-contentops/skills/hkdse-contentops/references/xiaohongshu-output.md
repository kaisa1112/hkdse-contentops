## Content objective

Create content that helps a qualified DSE student or parent make one decision or take one next step. Every asset needs:

- one audience;
- one specific problem;
- one useful promise;
- one evidence basis;
- one easy interaction;
- one compliant next action.

Write like a knowledgeable Mandarin-speaking teacher, not a brochure. Use short paragraphs, concrete situations, and restrained authority.

## Graphic-post contract

Return all fields:

1. content angle and target audience;
2. cover copy in one or two short lines;
3. one natural title no longer than 20 Chinese characters;
4. page-by-page copy and layout for 6–8 pages;
5. publish-ready body;
6. one easy comment question;
7. exactly five relevant hashtags;
8. one A/B cover or hook variable when optimization is requested.

Recommended page sequence:

1. Cover: name the decision or pain.
2. Recognition: show the common wrong approach without shaming.
3–5. Framework: one point per page with a concrete example.
6. Action tool: checklist, table, or decision path.
7. Summary: one sentence to remember.
8. Interaction: one low-friction question when an eighth page is useful.

Do not fill pages with generic definitions. A designer should be able to build the post without inventing missing copy.

## Graphic design system

Default to a 3:4 canvas such as 1242×1660 px. Treat this as a production recommendation, not a platform rule.

Use a trustworthy education style:

- navy or dark blue for authority;
- teal for structure;
- warm yellow for one emphasis;
- off-white background for readability;
- one sans-serif Chinese font family;
- generous margins and one conclusion per page.

Recommend phone-readable hierarchy:

- cover title: large, two lines maximum;
- inner-page heading: short and high contrast;
- body: three to six short lines or a compact table;
- accent: highlight only decision words, not whole paragraphs.

Use diagrams only when they clarify a process, comparison, timeline, or decision tree. Do not decorate with fake score reports, fabricated student portraits, or irrelevant campus stock photos.

## Short-video contract

Return all fields:

1. content angle and audience;
2. cover copy in one or two lines;
3. one title no longer than 20 Chinese characters;
4. 0–3 second hook;
5. 30–60 second verbatim Mandarin script;
6. shot, gesture, prop, or subtitle cues by beat;
7. publish-ready caption;
8. one easy comment question;
9. exactly five relevant hashtags;
10. optional A/B hook when optimization is requested.

Use this rhythm:

- 0–3s: identify audience and conflict;
- 3–12s: show why the common approach fails;
- 12–45s: deliver two to four concrete steps;
- final 5–10s: summarize and ask one answerable question.

Keep the spoken script natural. Do not make the teacher read headings, hashtags, citations, or long written sentences aloud.

## Hooks and titles

Use evidence-supported angles:

- pain: `刷题很多，为什么还是没有进步？`
- decision: `DSE选科先别问哪科容易`
- misconception: `背范文不等于会写作文`
- checklist: `中五升中六先做这4步`
- parent communication: `别再只问孩子学了多久`
- current response: `这项新安排影响哪类DSE家庭？`

Avoid fake urgency, universal outcomes, excessive punctuation, and fear language. Replace “死亡组合” with a conditional analysis such as “这类组合对哪些学生负担更大”.

## Captions, hashtags, and comments

Start the caption by deepening the hook. Use short paragraphs and one concrete takeaway. End with one easy question, not a vague “你怎么看”.

Use exactly five hashtags per post or video:

- one broad category: `#香港DSE`;
- one topic: `#DSE选科`;
- one audience or stage: `#DSE家长` or `#中五升中六`;
- one scenario: `#DSE备考`;
- one intent: `#香港升学` or another directly relevant term.

Do not stuff unrelated trending tags or attach a year unless the current year is necessary to the verified claim.

Good comment prompts ask for one or two safe fields:

- `你现在是中四、中五还是中六？`
- `最纠结哪一门选修科？`
- `你最常见的是知识、审题还是时间问题？`

Do not ask minors to post names, schools, scores, phone numbers, or contact details publicly.

## Acquisition handoff

Connect content to the next in-platform step:

- search post → related diagnostic post;
- diagnostic post → scheduled livestream;
- livestream → actual platform-approved consultation or course component;
- comments → next FAQ post or video;
- verified service experience → consented, non-fabricated feedback content.

If no approved service path exists, end with save, follow, comment, or livestream reminder. Never invent a lead form, product card, private message service, phone number, WeChat, or WhatsApp route.
