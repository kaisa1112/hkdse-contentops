## Positioning and profile

Define one sentence:

`幫助[具體香港DSE人群]將[複雜問題]變成[可執行結果]。`

Default neutral-media example:

`幫助香港F4–F6學生，將溫書、操卷、選科同JUPAS變成可以即刻執行嘅方法。`

Return a searchable display name, audience promise, three content categories, one real approved next action, profile-image direction, pinned-post roles, and Highlight purposes.

Use three pinned posts with different jobs:

1. `你會喺呢個Account得到乜`: positioning and audience;
2. `最值得收藏嘅方法`: strongest evergreen framework or toolkit;
3. `最新重點／服務`: current campaign, official update, event, or actual supplied offer.

Do not use all three pins for sales creatives.

Recommended Highlights:

`F4入門｜F5追分｜F6操卷｜中文｜英文｜Math｜選修科｜JUPAS｜工具｜學生Q&A`

Use only categories the account can maintain.

## Audience and content pillars

Start with this adjustable mix:

- 40% study diagnosis, answer methods, and exam execution;
- 20% subject-specific content;
- 15% JUPAS, subject choice, policy, and progression decisions;
- 15% student emotion, procrastination, confidence, and parent communication;
- 10% community Q&A, comment replies, tools, service explanation, and user-supplied proof.

Build clusters with one discovery Reel, one saveable carousel, one Story poll/quiz, one comment-reply or FAQ Reel, and one Highlight or approved destination when useful.

## Publishing system

Recommend a sustainable start, not a guarantee: two carousels, two Reels, Stories on active days, one FAQ derivative per week, and Instagram Live only when staffing and topic justify it.

Test at least two local-time windows using account data. Never present a universal best time.

For every calendar row provide date/weekday, stage, topic/discovery phrase, format, funnel purpose, CTA, verification need, Story support, and reuse plan. Use one campaign theme across formats without duplicating copy.

## Stories, Highlights, and community

Use Stories for a recognition poll, concept quiz, answer frame, feed handoff, safe question box, and an actual countdown/link when available. Turn repeated questions into named clusters and pin comments that add clarification or reveal the next question.

Do not manufacture engagement with fake accounts, engagement pods, copied comments, or fabricated questions.

## Acquisition funnel and DM qualification

Use:

`Reels discovery → profile promise → carousel save/share → Stories/Highlights trust → approved DM/profile-link action → qualified conversation → actual service/resource → consented feedback → FAQ content`

Design cold assets for recognition, warm assets for frameworks/examples/official explanation, and high-intent assets for actual supplied service fit, process, limitations, schedule, and price.

When an approved DM path exists, ask only for current stage, subject/decision topic, main non-identifying difficulty, desired outcome/time horizon, and preferred approved next step.

Do not request a minor's full name, school, exact score report, phone number, or private family history publicly. Use profile links, forms, booking pages, or WhatsApp only when supplied and approved.

For a monthly plan, return positioning, audience, four pillars, weekly themes, calendar, format roles, acquisition path, dashboard, and next-month decisions. Adapt to supplied brand/course/team facts; otherwise label assumptions.

## Diagnosis and experiments

Inspect topic, format, date/time, cover, hook, caption, CTA, follower/non-follower reach, Reel retention/watch/completion/shares/saves/profile visits/follows, carousel saves/shares/profile visits/follows, Story exits/responses/link taps, qualified DMs, profile-link actions, and the account's recent median.

Map constraints:

- low non-follower reach: packaging, opening, originality, or discovery fit;
- low Reel retention: slow opening, self-introduction, excess context, or delayed value;
- low carousel entry: vague cover or dense design;
- low saves/shares: weak action value or examples;
- low follows: unclear promise or no cluster;
- high reach but low qualified action: broad audience or weak handoff;
- low DM conversion: unclear scope, premature selling, or friction;
- zero comments: question too broad, personal, difficult, or unrelated.

Test one variable: cover wording, first-two-second hook, student/parent framing, visual opening, worked example/list format, CTA, or Reel/carousel order. Compare with the account's own baseline and decide repeat, expand, revise, or stop.

## Seasonal and trust rules

Map content to school-term transitions, F4 subject choice, F5-to-F6 summer, SBA/mock periods, exam preparation, JUPAS stages, results, and post-results decisions. Verify dates every year.

Do not use stale proof in a current profile. Replace an old result with current verified proof or clearly date and contextualize it.

For Instagram Live, define the topic, takeaway, 30-second opening, three interaction questions, moderation, Story countdown, replay/clip plan, and approved next action. Do not stream private records, copyrighted papers, guaranteed outcomes, or fabricated cases.
